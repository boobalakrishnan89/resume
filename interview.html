✅ FULL VERSION — 2 MINUTES

For Detailed Technical Interviews

"Hi, I'm BoobalaKrishnan Gopinathan. I'm a Senior QA Engineer and Lead QA with over 10 years of experience in Quality Assurance, specializing in Test Automation and AI-driven QA processes.

I started my career as a Test Engineer at Citiair in Chennai, where I built a strong foundation in manual testing, test case design, and defect management. I then moved to Infosys, where I transitioned into automation — working on enterprise-scale projects and learning Selenium and Java.

My career took a significant leap at ClearTax, where I spent over 3 years as a Senior QA Engineer in the FinTech space. There, I built and maintained CI/CD-integrated automation suites using Selenium and RestAssured, led API testing, conducted mobile testing across Android and iOS, and started mentoring junior engineers.

I then briefly worked at Simpl, another FinTech company focused on payments, where I contributed to their web automation framework and tested merchant integration and payment transaction flows.

For the past 2+ years, I've been at Onsurity as a Lead QA, where I lead a team of 8 QA engineers across the HealthTech domain. This is where I've had the most impact — I built 3 automation frameworks from scratch (Playwright for web, RestAssured for API, Appium for mobile), pioneered an AI-first QA strategy using Claude AI, GitHub Copilot, and JIRA MCP, built a Bug Tracking Utility and QA Dashboard, and a Database Validation Utility for automated data verification.

I hold a Master's degree in Computer Science from Anna University, and I'm now looking for a role where I can bring my leadership, framework-building expertise, and AI-driven QA innovation to drive quality strategy at an organizational level."

  • Structure: Past → Present → Future (where you came from, what you're doing now, what you want next)

  • Tone: Confident but not arrogant, conversational not rehearsed
